**Wandro 2.0** completes the game loop: real discoveries checked by the server, coins, a Store,
a social feed, Find-My-style walking, and a desktop app (roadmap Phases 3–8).

**Get it:** download the installer for your computer below (Windows `.exe`, macOS `.dmg`, Linux
`.AppImage`), or open the web app linked in the repository's About section and click
**Install app**. Without a backend it runs in demo mode with sample Sintra places.

> The installers aren't code-signed yet. Windows will show a SmartScreen warning (More info → Run
> anyway), and on macOS right-click the app → Open the first time.

## What's new

### 🧭 Walk to your next adventure (Find-My style)

- Your octopus marks where you are, with a pulsing accuracy circle.
- **Guide me** shows distance, walking time and how close you are, from _On your way_ to
  _You're here!_, with **Directions in Google Maps**.
- A nudge appears when a hidden gem is within 150 m.
- In the browser and desktop app (demo mode), walk with **WASD / arrow keys** or the on-screen pad.

### 🪙 Coins and the Store

- Discoveries pay coins (hidden gems pay up to 5×, with a +50 pioneer bonus). The **daily
  challenge pays double**, and completing a collection pays +200.
- **Store:** the 🪔 **incense trail** gives your octopus a glowing circle and draws a guiding line
  to your next adventure (30 min or 2 h). Octopus skins and hats are yours forever.
- Coins are only earned by playing. There's no subscription, and spending never lowers your rank.

### ✅ Real discoveries, verified by the server

- The server checks the geofence, GPS accuracy, a 2-minute stay (on its own clock), mock
  locations, impossible speed and rate limits.
- Raw location pings are deleted after each check-in.
- Flagged check-ins go to moderators.
- Earn levels, daily streaks and 12 badges.

### 👥 Explore together

- An Instagram-style feed of discoveries, with likes and optional photos. Location data is
  stripped from photos.
- Follow explorers. Private accounts approve their followers.
- Leaderboards for friends, Sintra, global and this week.
- Report and block, a moderation screen, and place suggestions that moderators approve as new
  missions.

### 🔒 Your data

- Settings let you make your account private, set a daily reminder, **download your data**
  (JSON) and **delete your account**.
- A plain-language privacy and community guidelines page.

### 🖥️ Desktop

- Windows, macOS and Linux apps, plus an installable web app.

## Under the hood

- Four new database migrations:
  - check-ins and scoring
  - social, collections and moderation
  - privacy export and delete
  - the store
- All of it runs in transactional `SECURITY DEFINER` functions behind row-level security.
- Tests:
  - 111 database tests
  - 32 shared-package tests and 7 importer tests
  - 39 app tests
  - 4 desktop tests
- The full flow was also checked end to end in a browser and in the Electron app.
- Fixed an infinite render loop on Home, caught while testing the desktop build.

## Known limitations

- The native Mapbox map, Google and Apple sign-in and a live Supabase deployment still need your
  accounts and keys (see the setup issue).
- Push notifications for friend activity aren't built yet. The daily reminder is a local
  notification.
- Store items and place names are English-only for now.
